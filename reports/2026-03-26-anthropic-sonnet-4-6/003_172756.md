## Hypothesis

P100 bottleneck is too few optimizer steps (33 in 5 min). Reducing TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE 2^19→2^17 gives 4x more steps. Also reduce WARMDOWN_RATIO 0.5→0.2 so less time is wasted on LR decay.

## Changes

- `TOTAL_BATCH_SIZE`: 2^19 → 2^17 (grad_accum: 16 → 4)
- `WARMDOWN_RATIO`: 0.5 → 0.2
