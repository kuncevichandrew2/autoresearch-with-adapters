## Result

Kept — val_loss 3.623 → 3.498. On RTX 3090, DEPTH=6 beats DEPTH=4 despite half the steps (3614 vs 7927). Model capacity now matters: 3614 steps is enough for 16.9M params to learn.

Key insight: on P100, DEPTH=6 got only 355 steps → 5.129. On RTX 3090, DEPTH=6 gets 3614 steps → 3.498. The "step count dominates" rule still holds, but RTX 3090 gives enough steps for larger models.
